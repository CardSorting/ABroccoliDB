maintainer. This file does not claim that either mark is registered or that a
formal trademark search has been completed.

The Apache License grants no trademark license. Factual references such as
“uses BroccoliDB” and accurate attribution of an unmodified or modified
distribution are allowed when they do not imply that the maintainer endorses,
certifies, sponsors, or is responsible for the third-party product.

Forks, hosted services, repackaged distributions, and competing databases
should use a distinct product name, package name, logo, and domain. Do not use
the BroccoliDB name or branding in a way that suggests an official build,
compatibility certification, or maintainer support without written permission.

change the software license or restrict rights that the Apache License grants
